# Automate EigenLayer Operations with Our New n8n Community Node

We're excited to announce the release of n8n-nodes-eigenlayer, a powerful new community node that brings EigenLayer's restaking infrastructure directly into your n8n workflows!

## The Challenge

EigenLayer is revolutionizing Ethereum's security layer through restaking, but interacting with its complex smart contracts and managing operations like delegation, EigenPods, and AVS registration typically requires extensive blockchain development knowledge. For teams managing multiple restaking positions or building services on top of EigenLayer, manual operations are time-consuming and error-prone.

## Introducing n8n-nodes-eigenlayer

Velocity BPA has developed this community node to bridge the gap between EigenLayer's powerful infrastructure and n8n's visual automation platform. Now you can automate your entire EigenLayer workflow without writing a single line of code.

## Key Features

- **Restaking Operations**: Automate LST deposits and withdrawals with visual workflows
- **Delegation Management**: Programmatically manage operator delegations at scale
- **EigenPods Integration**: Monitor and manage your EigenPod operations seamlessly
- **AVS Registration**: Streamline Active Validator Service registrations and updates
- **Real-time Monitoring**: Trigger workflows based on EigenLayer events and state changes

## Perfect For

- DeFi teams managing restaking strategies
- Node operators handling multiple delegations
- AVS providers automating registration workflows
- Protocol developers building on EigenLayer
- Investment DAOs tracking restaking positions

## Getting Started

Installation is simple. In your n8n instance, run:


npm install n8n-nodes-eigenlayer


Restart n8n, and you'll find the EigenLayer node in your node palette, ready to drag and drop into your workflows.
